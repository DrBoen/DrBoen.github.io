Ways to Improve

Based on your results, here are some suggestions for improvement:

  • Review 'Sometimes' items: These are your best opportunities for quick wins. Think about specific situations where you could consciously apply these practices more often.
  • Focus on 'Rarely/Never' items: Pick one or two of these practices to focus on for the next month. Seek out resources, ask for feedback from a trusted colleague, or find a mentor who excels in this area.
  • Practice Active Listening: In your next conversation, make a conscious effort to paraphrase what the other person said to ensure you understand them before responding.
  • Seek Feedback: Ask a supervisor or peer for their perspective on one of the areas you'd like to improve. For example: "I'm working on being more proactive in my communication. Is there a recent situation where I could have done better?"
  • Set SMART Goals: For an area of improvement, set a goal that is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. For example: "For the next two weeks, I will respond to all emails within 24 hours."
